What is Chemistry?	Chemistry is the science of molecules and their transformations. It is the branch of science that studies the preparation, properties, structure, and reactions of material substances.
Who was Acharya Kanda?	Acharya Kanda, born in 600 BCE, was the first proponent of the 'atomic theory' in India. He formulated the theory of 'Paramãnu' (indivisible particles) which are eternal, indestructible, spherical, suprasensible, and in motion.
What is the primary purpose of the Study Assistant app?	Study Assistant is a native desktop application designed to search local notes using semantic search and AI-powered retrieval. Its purpose is to generate answers grounded in the user's study material instead of the general internet.
Differentiate between precision and accuracy in measurements.	Precision refers to the closeness of various measurements for the same quantity. Accuracy is the agreement of a particular measured value with the true value of the result.
How does Study Assistant generate answers from notes?	Study Assistant searches notes using keyword matching and semantic embeddings to retrieve relevant chunks. These chunks are then sent to a selected Large Language Model (LLM) to generate an answer grounded in the user's notes.
